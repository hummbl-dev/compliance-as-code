# v0.1 Boundary: Compliance-as-Code

This repository is an executable v0.1 packet for **compliance evidence as code**.

## Scope

- Represent control mappings, evidence packets, attestations, and obligations as machine-checkable source.
- Keep compliance examples minimal, reviewable, and non-sensitive.
- Provide a practical structure for audit-readiness without asserting legal conclusions.

## Non-canon posture

This repository is `seed`/`v0.1-draft` only until explicitly adopted by HUMMBL authority.

## Boundaries

- Do not provide legal advice or create legal claims.
- Do not publish private audit artifacts or client-specific compliance secrets.
- Do not own final enforcement mechanics; governance remains in `governance-as-code`.
- Do not define security implementation standards; delegate to `security-as-code`.

## Initial practical user

Engineering/security/platform teams that need auditable, lightweight evidence packets for:

- control definitions,
- evidence status,
- review obligations,
- exception handling.

## Required packet pieces

- `docs/v0.1-boundary.md` (this file)
- `schemas/compliance-as-code-v0.1.json` (schema contract)

- `fixtures/invalid/compliance-evidence-v0.1.invalid.json` (negative fixture)
